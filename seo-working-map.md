# Pequlieer SEO Working Map
Updated: 13 October 2026

## Current setup and principles
- Live site currently uses GitHub Pages: https://meeieks.github.io/pequlieer/
- Primary geographic focus: Cape Town, Western Cape, South Africa.
- Business model: hands-on service work within Pequlieer's own skills and experience, plus coordination of suitable local providers for other requests.
- Do not imply every category is always available, that every provider is employed by Pequlieer, or that every provider is independently verified.
- Avoid doorway pages, keyword stuffing, fake reviews, invented business details, or separate thin pages for every keyword.
- Google says special AI files such as llms.txt are not needed for Google Search or its generative AI features. Prioritise accessible, helpful HTML content, accurate structured data and internal links.

## Information-gain stack for service content
For each future service page, include genuinely useful details rather than generic claims:
1. The actual task and who it suits.
2. What information a customer should provide to get a useful response.
3. What affects scope, timing and price (without publishing invented price promises).
4. What should be confirmed before work starts.
5. Relevant local context for Cape Town, only where accurate.
6. Clear distinction between work Pequlieer does itself and work handled by a provider.
7. Availability caveat and a clear enquiry next step.
8. First-hand examples, original photos or real process details when available; never fabricate case studies or credentials.

- Homepage footer -> About Pequlieer, HTML Sitemap, Privacy Notice and Join the Network.
- About Pequlieer -> relevant service categories, Request a Service and HTML Sitemap.
- Request a Service -> category list and homepage service categories.
- Join the Service Network -> homepage and privacy notice.
- HTML Sitemap -> every important public page.
- Add contextual links between future service pages only where genuinely relevant; avoid repeating the same exact-match anchor unnaturally.

## Structured-data and technical notes
- Homepage Organization JSON-LD is descriptive, not a guarantee of a rich result or ranking.
- Do not add a street address, opening hours, price range, reviews, credentials or social profiles until verified and publicly available.
- Keep canonical URLs, sitemap URLs and internal links aligned with the live domain. When a custom domain is purchased and configured, update these together and check redirects before asking Google to reindex.
- Submit the XML sitemap in Google Search Console and inspect important pages after publication.
- The HTML sitemap is for people and can also aid discovery; the XML sitemap remains the machine-readable sitemap.
