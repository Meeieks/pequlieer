ABOUT PEQULIEER

Practical help, connected locally.

Pequlieer is a Cape Town local service and coordination business. Customers can explain what they need in one place. Pequlieer carries out work that fits its own skills and experience and coordinates suitable local providers for other requests.

Business at a glance

  • Business name: Pequlieer.
  • Business model: Local service delivery and coordination.
  • Primary service area: Cape Town, Western Cape, South Africa.
  • How to start: Submit an enquiry and discuss the job before agreeing on next steps.
  • Provider network: Independent service providers may be considered based on their service, experience, service area and relevant evidence.

Service categories

  • Home & Property: enquiries about cleaning, gardening, plumbing, handyman work and property maintenance.
  • Moving & Physical Help: enquiries about moving, lifting, deliveries and practical labour.
  • Digital & Technology: enquiries about websites, devices and computer support.
  • Education & Tutoring: enquiries about lessons, homework and learning support.
  • Repairs & Metalwork: enquiries about welding, fabrication and general repairs.
  • Events & Catering: enquiries about food, event setup and event support.
  • Family & Childcare: enquiries about babysitting, childcare and family support.
  • Tourism & Visitors: enquiries about local visitor assistance and related services.
  • Something Else: other requests that may be discussed individually.

How the process works

  1. The customer describes the service needed and the general location.
  2. Pequlieer discusses the job, the practical details and the next step.
  3. Pequlieer considers whether it can do the work within its own skills and experience or whether a suitable local provider may be needed.
  4. Before proceeding, the customer and the person doing the work should confirm scope, price, timing, materials and any relevant qualifications.

An enquiry is not a confirmed booking or a guarantee that a provider is available. Provider suitability depends on the particular job. Customers should clarify any qualifications or arrangements important to them before proceeding.
