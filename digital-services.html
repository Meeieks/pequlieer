WHAT I CAN HELP WITH

Defined work. Clear expectations.

01

Simple business websites

A clean, mobile-friendly site that explains your services, shows how to contact you and gives customers a clear next step.

Suitable for a small business starting its online presence or replacing an outdated basic site.

02

Website updates and maintenance

Agreed changes to service descriptions, contact details, images, page content and other small updates on an existing website.

Access, platform limitations, backups and the exact scope are discussed before work starts.

03

Social media post support

Help preparing captions and a simple posting plan, or preparing and publishing agreed posts using material supplied or approved by you.

Photography, video creation, paid advertising and replying to customers are separate tasks unless explicitly included.

04

Online-presence review

A practical review of how your business describes its services online, with prioritised suggestions for clearer content, contact routes and basic website discoverability.

A review is guidance, not a guarantee of search rankings, leads or sales.
