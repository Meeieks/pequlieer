ABOUT PEQULIEER

Making local service connections simpler.

Pequlieer is a Cape Town-based service coordination business connecting people who need practical help with suitable local service providers. We provide hands-on services where they match our skills and experience, and work in partnership with trusted individuals and businesses to meet other needs.

Every request is discussed individually so that the work required, responsibilities, pricing and timing are clear before anything is agreed.

FAQ

Questions? Start here.

What services can I ask about?

Pequlieer currently focuses on cleaning and household assistance, moving help, small-business website and digital support, English tutoring and learning support, events/catering/decor, and babysitting/childcare. Moving and provider-delivered services depend on suitable availability and are confirmed individually.

Does sending an enquiry book a service?

No. An enquiry is just the first conversation. You are not committed to proceed, and no booking is confirmed until the details, price, timing and responsibilities have been agreed.

How are price and payment agreed?

We discuss the work required, price, timing and who will provide the service before you commit. Materials, travel, payment arrangements and any additional work or costs are clarified and agreed before proceeding.

Can I join the Pequlieer network?

Yes. If you have relevant skills and experience, use the For Providers page to describe your services and share examples of your work where available. We review expressions of interest individually; applying does not guarantee acceptance or work.
