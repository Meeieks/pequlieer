FAQ

Questions? Start here.

What areas do you cover?

Pequlieer currently focuses on Cape Town and surrounding areas where we have suitable connections.

Do I need to know exactly which service I need?

No. Tell us what you're trying to accomplish and we'll help you figure out where to start.

How much does a service cost?

Pricing depends on the service provider and the work required. We'll help connect you so the details can be discussed before work begins.

Can I request something that isn't listed?

Yes. Choose “Something Else” or describe your request in the enquiry form.

Are service providers verified?

We're building our local network and will work toward clear provider checks. Customers should confirm pricing, qualifications and arrangements before work begins.
