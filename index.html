ABOUT PEQULIEER

Making local service connections simpler.

Pequlieer is a Cape Town local service and coordination business built around practical help and direct communication. We work with customers to understand what needs doing, and carry out jobs that fit our own skills, qualifications and experience.

For work that requires other expertise, we look for a suitable local provider and help clarify the arrangements. We’re building our network carefully, so every request is considered individually and availability is not guaranteed.

FAQ

Questions? Start here.

What areas do you cover?

Pequlieer currently focuses on Cape Town and surrounding areas. Whether we can help depends on the service and the connections available for your request.

Do I need to know exactly which service I need?

No. Tell us what you're trying to accomplish and we'll help you figure out where to start.

How much does a service cost?

Costs depend on the type of work, the provider and any materials or other requirements. The service details, price and timing should be discussed before you agree to proceed.

Does sending an enquiry confirm a booking?

No. An enquiry is the first step, not a confirmed booking. We'll review what you need and look for a suitable connection, but availability is not guaranteed. Any arrangement should be confirmed after the details have been discussed.

Can I request something that isn't listed?

Yes. Choose “Something Else” or describe your request in the enquiry form. We don't have a provider for every possible service, but we'll try to find a suitable connection.

What if you can't find someone suitable?

We'll let you know if we can't find a suitable person in our network. Where possible, we'll try to explore another option, but we can't guarantee that a provider will be available.

Who receives my enquiry?

Your enquiry opens as a message to Pequlieer in WhatsApp. You can review it before sending. We'll use the details to understand your request and discuss possible next steps with you.

Are service providers verified?

We review the information providers share with us, including relevant experience, service area and examples of previous work where available. Depending on the service, we may ask follow-up questions or request relevant qualifications. This review helps us consider a potential connection; it is not an independent verification of every claim or a guarantee of future performance. Before proceeding, confirm the provider’s suitability, the exact work, price, timing and any important arrangements.

Does Pequlieer carry out the work itself?

Pequlieer helps clarify requests and connect customers with potentially suitable service providers. The provider carries out the service. Before work begins, make sure you understand who is doing the work, what is included, the price, timing, materials and how any changes will be handled.

How do I become a service provider?

Use the “For Providers” or “Join the Network” link to introduce your service and share your experience. Sending your details expresses interest; it does not guarantee acceptance or future work.
