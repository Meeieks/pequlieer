ABOUT PEQULIEER

Making local service connections simpler.

Pequlieer is a Cape Town local service and coordination business built around practical help and direct communication. We work with customers to understand what needs doing, and carry out jobs that fit our own skills, qualifications and experience.

For work that requires other expertise, we consider suitable local providers and help clarify the arrangements. We’re building our network carefully, so every request is considered individually and availability is not guaranteed.

We also provide small-business website and digital support, drawing on practical website projects and online-presence work. These projects are scoped separately from local service enquiries.

FAQ

Questions? Start here.

What services can I ask about?

Pequlieer handles enquiries for a range of practical services in Cape Town, including home and property work, moving help, technology, tutoring, repairs, catering and more. Browse the categories to see examples.

Who receives my enquiry?

Your enquiry comes to Pequlieer first. We’ll contact you to understand the job and, where appropriate, look for a local provider who may be a suitable fit. We only share relevant details as needed to discuss or arrange the requested service.

When will I know the price?

We’ll discuss the scope of the work and any materials, travel or other requirements before the price and next steps are agreed. If the job changes, discuss any additional work and cost before proceeding with it.

Does sending an enquiry book a service?

No. An enquiry is just the first conversation. You are not committed to proceed, and no booking is confirmed until the details, price, timing and responsibilities have been agreed.

Can I ask for website or digital support?

Yes. We offer defined small-business website and digital support, including simple websites, website updates, social media post support and online-presence reviews. Visit the Digital Support page to discuss a project.

How do you handle the price and payment?

Before work starts, we explain the proposed scope and price and clarify who is providing the service and how payment will be handled. Materials, travel, additional work and other costs should be agreed before you commit.

Can I join the Pequlieer network?

Yes. If you have relevant skills and experience, use the For Providers page to describe your services and share examples of your work where available. We review expressions of interest individually; applying does not guarantee acceptance or work.
