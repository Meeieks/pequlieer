YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Pequlieer helps people in Cape Town explain what service they need and seek a suitable local service provider. This notice explains how we handle personal information when you use our website, contact us, submit a service enquiry or express interest in joining our provider network.

Please note: Our website forms prepare a message for you to review in WhatsApp. Your enquiry is not sent to Pequlieer until you press Send in WhatsApp.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with Pequlieer, we may receive:

  • Customer enquiries: your name, phone number, optional email address, general area, service category, preferred timing and details you choose to provide about the job.
  • Provider expressions of interest: your name, optional business name, phone number, service category, services offered, service areas, experience or qualifications, and an optional website or examples of your work.
  • Follow-up and job records: information needed to clarify a request, communicate about availability, discuss a quote, coordinate an agreed job, follow up or maintain necessary business records.
  • Technical information: basic information that may be collected by the website host or services you use to access the site, according to their own settings and policies.

Please provide only information relevant to your enquiry. Do not include passwords, bank-card details, identity numbers, detailed security/access codes, or unnecessary sensitive information in the website form or WhatsApp message.

2. Why we use personal information

We use information to understand and respond to enquiries, contact you about your request, look for a potentially suitable and available provider, discuss the proposed service and next steps, coordinate work where an arrangement is agreed, assess provider applications, keep appropriate business records, protect our service and meet applicable legal obligations.

Sending an enquiry does not guarantee that a suitable provider will be found or that a booking will be made.

3. WhatsApp and other services

When you submit a customer or provider form, the website prepares a message and opens WhatsApp for you to review. You decide whether to send it. If you press Send, the information in the message is delivered to Pequlieer through WhatsApp.

WhatsApp and other third-party services may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Pequlieer does not control how those services operate. We also plan to use Notion for private enquiry and job tracking; information entered there is subject to Notion’s own service terms and privacy practices.

4. When information may be shared

We do not publish customer enquiries. If it is useful to assess or carry out a request, we may need to share relevant details with a prospective or selected service provider. We aim to share only what is reasonably necessary. Where identifiable customer details need to be shared, we will explain the proposed sharing and seek your agreement where appropriate before doing so.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect rights or safety, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We do not sell personal information.

5. How we protect information

We aim to limit access to enquiry records, keep our accounts and devices secure, avoid unnecessary copies of conversations, and share information only with people who need it for the relevant request. No online service or method of storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Please contact us if you believe information you shared with Pequlieer has been exposed or sent to the wrong person.

6. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including handling enquiries, managing agreed work, maintaining required business records, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations. Retention needs may differ for enquiries that do not proceed and jobs that are completed. When information is no longer needed and we are not required or otherwise permitted to keep it, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or remove details that identify you.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us what personal information we hold about you, request correction of information that is inaccurate, raise an objection or concern about how it is used, or request deletion where appropriate. We may need to verify your identity and may need to retain some records where the law allows or requires it.

8. Children’s information

Please do not send unnecessary personal information about a child. If a request involves tutoring, childcare or another service involving a child, provide only the details needed to understand the service. A parent or authorised guardian should make the enquiry where appropriate.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as Pequlieer’s processes or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page, with the date of the latest update shown above.

10. Contact Pequlieer about privacy

For privacy questions, requests about your personal information or concerns about how it has been handled, contact Pequlieer through our WhatsApp contact and say that your message concerns privacy.

Pequlieer operates in Cape Town, South Africa. This notice is intended to explain our current approach and is not a guarantee of legal compliance. We will review it as our business processes develop.
