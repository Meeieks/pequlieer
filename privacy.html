YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Pequlieer collects and uses personal information when you make a service enquiry, communicate with us or express interest in working with us. Pequlieer is a Cape Town-based service coordination business operating in South Africa.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Before you send an enquiry: Website forms prepare a message for you to review in WhatsApp. The form does not send the message automatically. Your enquiry reaches Pequlieer only if you choose to press Send in WhatsApp.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Pequlieer is responsible for the personal information it collects and uses for its service coordination and provider-partnership activities. You can contact us about privacy using the WhatsApp contact details at the end of this notice. We will route privacy requests to the person responsible for handling them.

2. Information we may collect

We collect information you choose to provide, as well as limited technical information that may be handled by the services used to operate or access the website. Depending on your interaction with us, this may include:

  • Customer enquiries: your name, phone number, optional email address, general area, service requested, preferred timing and details needed to understand the job.
  • Provider partnership enquiries: your name, optional business name, contact details, services offered, service areas, experience, relevant qualifications and any website or work examples you choose to share.
  • Communication and job records: information needed to clarify a request, discuss a quote, coordinate an agreed service, record relevant costs or arrangements, follow up and handle questions or disputes.
  • Technical information: information that may be processed by the website host or other platforms, such as basic access or device data, according to their own services and policies.

Please share only information relevant to your request. Do not send passwords, bank-card details, identity numbers, access codes or unnecessary sensitive information through our forms or messages.

3. How and why we use information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, contact you about your request, understand the work required, assess whether we can provide the service ourselves or identify a potentially suitable local provider, discuss scope and pricing, coordinate work where an arrangement is agreed, consider provider partnership enquiries, maintain appropriate business records, address problems and meet legal obligations.

We ask for information relevant to these purposes and aim not to collect more than we reasonably need. If you choose not to provide details needed to assess a request, we may be unable to progress it. Sending an enquiry does not guarantee a provider will be available or that a booking will be made.

4. WhatsApp, website hosting and other platforms

When you use a website enquiry form, it prepares a message and opens WhatsApp for you to review. You decide whether to send it. If you press Send, WhatsApp processes and delivers the message under its own terms and privacy practices.

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider and any other platform we use may process technical information under their own terms. We may use internal record-keeping systems to organise enquiries and job details, with access limited to people who need the information for the relevant work.

Some service providers may process or store information in countries outside South Africa. Their handling of information, including any international transfers, is subject to their own practices and applicable terms. We aim to share only information needed for the purpose and to use reasonable care when choosing and using these services.

5. When information may be shared

We do not publish your enquiry or sell personal information. To assess or arrange a service, we may need to share relevant details with a prospective or selected service provider. Where we need to share information that identifies you, we will explain what is to be shared and why, and seek your agreement where appropriate before sharing it.

We may also disclose information when required or authorised by law, or where reasonably necessary to protect safety, address suspected wrongdoing, resolve a dispute or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

6. How we protect information

We take reasonable practical steps to protect personal information, including limiting access to records, securing the accounts and devices we use, and avoiding unnecessary copies or disclosures. Information may be handled by third-party platforms, and no website, messaging service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. If you believe information you shared with Pequlieer has been exposed or sent to the wrong person, please contact us promptly.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including responding to enquiries, managing agreed work, keeping appropriate business and financial records, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations. The period may differ for enquiries that do not proceed and jobs that are completed. When information is no longer needed and there is no lawful reason to retain it, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or remove identifying details.

8. Your privacy rights and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask whether we hold personal information about you, request access to it, ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete details, object to certain processing, or request deletion where appropriate. Where processing relies on your consent, you may withdraw that consent; this does not make earlier processing unlawful and may affect whether we can continue handling your request. We may ask for reasonable proof of identity before acting on a request and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.

You may also raise a concern with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your personal information has been handled unlawfully or your rights have not been respected.

9. Information about children

Please do not send unnecessary personal information about a child. If a request involves tutoring, babysitting or another service involving a child, provide only the details needed to understand the request. A parent or authorised guardian should make the enquiry where appropriate. We will take care when handling information about children and will only process it as permitted by applicable law.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our services, tools or processes change, or when needed to reflect legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.

11. Contact Pequlieer about privacy

For a privacy question, access or correction request, deletion request or concern about how your information has been handled, contact us through Pequlieer on WhatsApp at 068 122 6185 and begin your message with “Privacy request”. Please do not include identity documents or other sensitive information unless we explain a secure and necessary way to provide it.
